How to Earn Money Watching Videos in Nigeria with Jovia

By Jovia Network · 24 October 2026 · 3 min read

How Jovia Network's video tasks work: ₦100 per active second, sessions of 20 seconds to 2 minutes, a ₦4,000 daily limit, and why time only counts while the video plays.

In this article
  1. How video tasks pay
  2. Step by step
  3. Why time only counts while the video plays
  4. Withdrawing what you earn

How video tasks pay

Video streaming is one of the task modules in the Jovia dashboard. It pays ₦100 for every active second of a session. Sessions last between 20 seconds and 2 minutes, and you choose the length before you start.

  • A 20-second session pays ₦2,000.
  • The daily limit for videos is ₦4,000 ($4). Once you reach it, the module reopens at midnight, Lagos time.
  • If you choose a session longer than the limit you have left, the dashboard shows the smaller amount you will actually receive before you start.

Step by step

  1. Sign in and open the Tasks tab.
  2. Choose Celebrity Video Streaming and pick a video.
  3. Set your countdown, then press Start.
  4. Keep the dashboard open while the video plays. When the timer reaches zero, the reward goes to your wallet.

Why time only counts while the video plays

The timer pauses if you pause the video, switch to another tab or close the page. Your dashboard checks in with our server every few seconds, and the server decides when a session is complete. That keeps the rewards fair and stops anyone from earning without actually watching.

Withdrawing what you earn

Video earnings go into the same wallet as the rest of your rewards. You can withdraw to your saved bank account from the Wallet tab. Withdrawals are usually paid within 24 to 48 hours on business days.
