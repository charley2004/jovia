01 Who We Are

Jovia Network ("Jovia", "we", "us") operates the digital task platform at joviaplatform.ng. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal data when you use our website, dashboard and services, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

02 Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — first and last name, email address, phone number, country, password and, if used, a referral code.
  • Payment and withdrawal details — activation payment records, and the bank name, account number and account name you add for withdrawals.
  • Marketplace content — product listings, descriptions and images you upload.
  • Communications — messages you send to our support channels.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — task sessions, timer durations, completions, earnings and referral activity, used to calculate your balance.
  • Device and technical data — IP address, browser type, operating system and device type, used for security and fraud prevention.

What we do not collect: we do not collect your BVN, NIN or card numbers, and we do not access your social media or streaming accounts.

03 How We Use Your Information

  • To create and run your account and dashboard.
  • To verify your activation payment and process withdrawals to your bank account.
  • To record task sessions and credit task, referral and bonus earnings.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, multiple accounts and automated (bot) activity.
  • To respond to support requests and send important account notices.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We do not use your personal data for advertising targeting and we do not sell it.

04 Legal Basis for Processing

Under the NDPA we process your personal data on these lawful bases:

  • Contract — to provide the account and services you signed up for.
  • Legal obligation — to keep financial records and respond to lawful requests.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the platform secure and prevent fraud.
  • Consent — for anything optional, such as marketing messages. You can withdraw consent at any time.

05 Sharing & Service Providers

We share personal data only where needed to run the platform:

  • Banks and payment processors — to verify activation payments and send withdrawals. [ Payment provider name(s) ]
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — to host the website, dashboard and database.
  • Support tools — the messaging channels you choose to contact us through.
  • AI Assistant (Google Gemini) — questions you type into the dashboard Assistant are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate an answer. We send only your question and general information about how Jovia works — never your name, contact details, balance or bank details. Google may use these questions to improve its products, so do not type personal information into the Assistant.
  • Authorities — where required by Nigerian law, court order or a lawful request.

Where a provider processes data outside Nigeria, we only use providers that offer the level of protection required by the NDPA.

06 Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • All traffic between your browser and joviaplatform.ng is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.
  • Staff access to member data is limited to those who need it for support, payments and fraud prevention.

We keep your personal data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as payment and tax records.

07 Your Rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact section below. We respond within the time limits set by the NDPA.

08 Cookies & Browser Storage

  • Session cookie — a secure, HTTP-only cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It holds no personal details, and signing out removes it.
  • Fonts — pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or retargeting. If we add analytics in future, we will update this section first.

09 Children's Privacy

Jovia Network is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10 Business Transfers

If Jovia Network is merged, acquired or sells its assets, member data may transfer to the new owner. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. When we make a material change, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify active members by email or a notice in the dashboard.
