Play Games and Earn Money in Nigeria: How Jovia Gameplay Pays

By Jovia Network · 24 October 2026 · 3 min read

Jovia Network's gameplay tasks pay ₦6,000 per completed session, win or lose. Here is how the countdown works, the daily limit and what happens if you stop playing.

In this article
  1. Paid for playtime, not for winning
  2. The games
  3. How a session works
  4. After the session

Paid for playtime, not for winning

Jovia's gameplay module rewards the time you spend playing, not your score. Complete a session and you earn ₦6,000, whether you win or lose. The daily limit for games is ₦6,000 ($6), which is one completed session a day.

The games

Games run inside your dashboard. They include Jovia Arcade classics such as Memory Match and Snake, with more added over time.

How a session works

  1. Open the Tasks tab and choose Interactive Gameplay.
  2. Pick a game and set a countdown of at least 30 seconds.
  3. Press Start and play until the timer reaches zero.

If you stop playing for 20 seconds, the timer pauses until you carry on. Switching tabs pauses it too. This keeps the reward tied to real play.

After the session

Your ₦6,000 is added to your wallet as soon as the session completes. You can see it in your wallet history and withdraw it to your bank account. Games reopen at midnight, Lagos time.
