Our Mission

Why We Built Jovia

Most people spend hours a day streaming music and video, playing games, and browsing products online — time that generates advertising and engagement revenue for everyone except the person actually spending it.

Jovia Network solves this. We built a task-based platform where anyone can activate an account, set a countdown timer, take part in a partner activity, and earn a defined payout the moment the timer completes — withdrawn directly to a linked bank account.

Our one-time activation model (₦15,000, no monthly fees) means the platform stays accessible to anyone, not just people who can afford a recurring subscription. We believe the barrier to earning from your own time online should be your participation — not an ongoing bill.

Our Core Values

The principles that shape how Jovia Network is built and operated.

Transparency Statement

Is Jovia Network Legitimate?

We know platforms come and go. Here's what makes Jovia different, and what we want you to know before you join.

What Jovia Network is

  • A task-based rewards platform — you're paid for completing verified, timed digital actions: streaming, gameplay, and marketplace listings.
  • A one-time payment model — your ₦15,000 activation unlocks your account. There are no monthly renewals or hidden upgrade charges.
  • A real payment system — withdrawals go to your linked bank account within 24–48 hours of request.
  • A fully transparent earning structure — fixed, published rates per task type. No hidden multipliers, conditions or recruitment bonuses.

What Jovia Network is not

  • Not an investment scheme — we don't trade assets, hold deposits, or promise compounding financial returns.
  • Not a guaranteed salary — earnings depend on your own task participation, not a fixed passive payout.
  • Not a free giveaway portal — account activation requires the one-time ₦15,000 fee to cover platform provisioning and the welcome bonus credited back to you.
