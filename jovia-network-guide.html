Jovia Network in one minute

Jovia Network is a rewards platform for people in Nigeria and across Africa. You create a free account, activate it once with a ₦15,000 bank transfer, and then earn fixed amounts of naira for timed activities in your dashboard: watching videos, listening to music and playing games. You can also sell products on the Jovia Marketplace and take part in the weekly Friday Bonus. Everything you earn collects in your wallet, and you withdraw it to your own Nigerian bank account.

Three things make Jovia different from the “earn money online” sites you may have seen:

  • Published rates and daily limits. Every task has a fixed rate and a daily cap, shown before you start. The most anyone can earn from tasks is ₦12,000 a day.
  • No recruitment. There is no referral programme. Nobody is paid for signing up other people, and nobody earns from your activation fee.
  • A refund if we never activate you. If you pay and your account is never activated, you get your money back.

Dollar amounts on Jovia are paid in naira at $1 = ₦1,000. Earnings depend on the tasks you complete and are never guaranteed.

Who can join

Anyone aged 18 or over in one of the 54 African countries can create an account. Sign-up asks for your first and last name, email address, phone number, country and a password, and you accept the Terms. One account per person: duplicate accounts are closed.

What you need before you start

  • A phone or computer with a modern browser. Jovia runs in the browser; there's no app to install. Tasks only count while the dashboard is on screen, so a steady internet connection helps.
  • A bank account in your own name. You'll pay the activation fee from it, and withdrawals are paid to it. The name on the account should match the name you sign up with, which makes verification faster.
  • Telegram, to send your payment receipt to our verification team after you pay. You can install it before you start.
  • An email address you check. We send your activation confirmation, withdrawal confirmations and important account messages there.

Step 1: Create your free account

Go to Create Account and fill in the form. Creating the account costs nothing, and you land straight on your dashboard. Until you activate, the dashboard shows the activation screen instead of the task modules.

If you sign up but don't activate straight away, we send a few friendly reminder emails over the next week. Every reminder has an unsubscribe link, and they stop as soon as you activate.

Step 2: Activation — the ₦15,000 one-time fee

Activation is a single bank transfer. There is no monthly subscription, no upgrade package and no second payment to unlock withdrawals.

  1. Copy the bank details from your dashboard. The activation screen shows our bank name, account name and account number. Only ever pay to the details shown inside your own dashboard.
  2. Make the transfer from your banking app, then enter the name on the account you paid from (and the transfer reference, if you have one).
  3. Tap “I've made the transfer”. We then open Telegram so you can send your receipt to our verification team at @Jovia_Registration. Include your name, email and the amount you sent.
  4. Wait for verification. Our team checks every payment by hand, usually within 12 hours. Your dashboard updates by itself, and you get a notification and an email the moment your account is active.

Activation adds a ₦15,000 Welcome Smash Bonus to your wallet and unlocks every task module, the marketplace and the Friday Bonus.

Is there an activation code?

No. Jovia Network does not sell or use activation codes. If someone offers you a “Jovia activation code” or asks you to pay the fee to a personal account, they are not acting for us — report it through our Contact page.

If something goes wrong

  • We couldn't verify your payment: you'll see the reason on the activation screen and can submit the details again.
  • You tapped “I've made the transfer” by mistake: choose “I haven't paid yet — go back”.
  • You paid but your account was never activated: tap “Request a refund”. You are entitled to the full ₦15,000 back. Once an account is active, the fee is not refundable.

Step 3: The task timer dashboard

Every task runs on a countdown timer. Open the Tasks tab, choose a module and an item, set your duration and press Start. Before you start, the dashboard shows exactly what the session will pay.

The timer is fair to everyone because the server, not your phone, decides when a session is complete:

  • Time only counts while your dashboard is open and on screen. Switching to another tab or app pauses it.
  • For videos and music, time only counts while the media is actually playing.
  • In games, the timer pauses if you stop playing for 20 seconds.
  • You can run one timer at a time. Starting a new one ends the one before it.

When the timer reaches zero, the reward goes straight into your wallet and appears in your transaction history.

Selling and buying on the Jovia Marketplace

Selling: merchant upload verification

Activated members can list products from the Market tab: a title, category, price, quantity, description and a photo. Every upload goes through verification: our team reviews the listing before it goes live. When it's approved you earn ₦3,000, and the product becomes visible to other members. Use a real photo of the item, an honest description and a fair price — listings that don't match the item are rejected.

Buying: in full or in instalments

Purchases are paid from your Jovia wallet. Pay in full, or choose pay small-small: a 50% deposit now and the balance within 14 days, with the item reserved while you pay. If the balance isn't paid in time, the order is cancelled and your deposit goes back to your wallet.

Your money is held until delivery

The seller isn't paid when you buy. The money is held until you confirm the item arrived, or released automatically 7 days after the seller marks it shipped. If there's a problem with a shipped order, report it from your orders list and our team reviews it before any money moves.

The Friday Bonus

Every Friday at 12:00 (Lagos time) a number appears in your dashboard's Friday tab. The first 10 members to claim it win ₦10,000 ($10) each, added to their wallet straight away.

Your wallet and the local bank withdrawal window

The Wallet tab shows your balance and every credit and debit: Welcome Bonus, task rewards, marketplace earnings, Friday Bonus wins and withdrawals.

  1. Save your bank account once: bank name, account number and the name on the account.
  2. Request a withdrawal of any amount up to your balance.
  3. Get paid within the local bank withdrawal window of 24 to 48 hours on business days.

When a withdrawal is paid you get a notification and an email with the bank reference. If a withdrawal can't be sent, the full amount goes back to your wallet with the reason.

A first week, step by step

Here is how the first days usually go, using the daily limits above. These are the maximums for someone who completes every task, not a promise of income.

  • Day 1: sign up, transfer the ₦15,000 activation fee and send your receipt on Telegram. Once your account is verified, the ₦15,000 Welcome Smash Bonus is in your wallet.
  • Day 2: open the Tasks tab. A 20-second video session pays ₦2,000 and you can earn up to ₦4,000 from videos, one music session pays ₦2,000, and one completed game session pays ₦6,000 — up to ₦12,000 for the day.
  • Day 3: list something you want to sell. When our team approves the listing, ₦3,000 is added to your wallet.
  • Friday: be on your dashboard at 12:00. If you're one of the first 10 to claim the number, you win ₦10,000.
  • Any day: save your bank account and withdraw. Payment reaches your bank within 24 to 48 hours on business days.

Most members don't complete every task every day, and that's fine: there's no minimum activity, and your balance stays in your wallet until you withdraw it.

Stay safe

  • Only pay to the bank details shown inside your own dashboard.
  • Jovia staff will never ask for your password.
  • Ignore anyone promising guaranteed income or rewards for recruiting people in Jovia's name.

Quick answers

How long does activation take?

Usually within 12 hours of receiving your receipt on Telegram. Your dashboard updates by itself.

Can I earn without activating?

No. Tasks, the marketplace and the Friday Bonus unlock after activation.

Why didn't my timer move?

The dashboard was in the background, the video or music was paused, or a game sat idle for 20 seconds. Bring the dashboard back on screen and carry on.

How do I get help?
