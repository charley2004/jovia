What Is Jovia Network? How It Works, Fees and Daily Limits

By Jovia Network · 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

A plain guide to Jovia Network: the one-time ₦15,000 activation, how timed video, music and game tasks pay, the daily limits, the marketplace and how withdrawals work.

  1. The short version
  2. Joining and activation
  3. How the timed tasks pay
  4. The marketplace and the Friday Bonus
  5. Withdrawals

The short version

Jovia Network is a rewards platform for people in Africa. After a one-time activation, members earn small, fixed amounts for timed digital activities: watching videos, listening to music and playing games. They can also list products on the Jovia Marketplace and take part in a weekly Friday Bonus. Earnings collect in a wallet and can be withdrawn to a bank account.

There is no referral or recruitment programme. Nobody is paid for signing up other people. What you earn comes from your own activity.

Joining and activation

  • Sign up free with your name, email, phone number and country. All 54 African countries are supported.
  • Activate once for ₦15,000 by bank transfer. The payment screen in your dashboard shows the account details.
  • Our team checks the transfer and activates your account. Activation adds a ₦15,000 Welcome Smash Bonus to your wallet.
  • If you paid but your account was never activated, you can ask for a full refund from the dashboard.

How the timed tasks pay

Every task runs on a countdown timer. Time only counts while your dashboard is open, and for videos and music only while the media is actually playing. The server decides when a session is complete, so leaving the page does not earn anything.

TaskHow it paysDaily limit
Celebrity video streaming₦100 per active second (sessions of 20 seconds to 2 minutes)₦4,000 ($4)
Interactive gameplay₦6,000 per completed session, win or lose₦6,000 ($6)
Music streaming₦2,000 per 60-second session₦2,000 ($2)

That is at most ₦12,000 a day from tasks. Limits reset at midnight, Lagos time. Dollar figures are paid in naira at $1 = ₦1,000.

The marketplace and the Friday Bonus

Members can list products on the Jovia Marketplace. Each listing our team approves earns ₦3,000. Buyers pay from their wallet, in full or in instalments, and the seller is paid once the buyer confirms delivery.

Every Friday at 12:00 (Lagos time) a number appears in the dashboard. The first 10 members to claim it win ₦10,000 each.

Withdrawals

Save your bank account in the Wallet tab, then request a withdrawal. Withdrawals are paid to that account, usually within 24 to 48 hours on business days. Every credit and debit is listed in your wallet history.

What Jovia is not

Jovia is not an investment scheme and does not promise a salary. Earnings depend entirely on the tasks you complete, up to the daily limits above. Read our Terms and Privacy Policy before you join.
