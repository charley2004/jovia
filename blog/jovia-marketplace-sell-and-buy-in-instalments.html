Jovia Marketplace: Earn ₦3,000 per Listing and Buy in Instalments

By Jovia Network · 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

How the Jovia Marketplace works: ₦3,000 for every approved product listing, buying from your wallet in full or with a 50% deposit, and payment held until you confirm delivery.

In this article
  1. Sell: earn for every approved listing
  2. Buy: in full or with instalments
  3. Your money is held until delivery
  4. Tips for sellers

Sell: earn for every approved listing

Activated members can list products on the Jovia Marketplace from the Market tab: a title, category, price, quantity, description and a photo. Our team reviews each listing. When it is approved, you earn ₦3,000 and the product goes live for other members to buy.

Buy: in full or with instalments

Purchases are paid from your Jovia wallet. You can choose:

  • Pay in full at checkout, or
  • Pay small-small: a 50% deposit now and the balance within 14 days. The item is reserved for you while you pay.

If an instalment balance isn't paid within the 14 days, the order is cancelled and your deposit goes back to your wallet.

Your money is held until delivery

When you buy, the seller isn't paid straight away. The money is held until you confirm the item arrived. If you don't confirm, it is released to the seller automatically 7 days after they mark the order as shipped. If there's a problem with a shipped order, you can report it and our team will review it before any money moves.

Tips for sellers

  • Use a clear photo of the real item and an honest description.
  • Set a fair price and the correct quantity.
  • Mark orders as shipped promptly, so the release timer starts.
